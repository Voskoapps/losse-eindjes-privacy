Privacy Policy Losse Eindjes

Last updated on 5 October 2026

Losse Eindjes ("loose ends") is an Android app by Voskoapps. If you have a question about this policy or about the app, email info@vosko-apps.com.

In short: the app collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking software.

What the app stores

The app only stores what you put into it yourself: titles, notes, next steps, groups, dates, reminders and whether something is waiting on someone. This is kept in the app's storage on your device. The maker of the app cannot access it and never sees any of it.

What the app does not do

  • You do not need to create an account, and you never enter your name or email address.
  • The app sends nothing to the maker or to any other party.
  • It contains no advertising and no software that tracks how you use the app.
  • The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Notifications

When you set a reminder for an item, the app asks for permission to show notifications. The notification is scheduled and shown by your device itself; no server is involved. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings. The reminder then remains visible inside the app.

Backup copy

The "Make a copy" button creates a file containing your list and opens the Android share menu. You choose where that file goes, for example your email or your cloud storage. From that moment the rules of the service you choose apply.

If Android backup is switched on for your device, Android may include the app's data in the backup of your Google account. Android handles this, not the app. You manage it in your device settings, and Google's privacy policy applies to it.

Purchase

You buy the app through Google Play. Google handles the payment; which data Google uses for it and shares with the maker is described in Google's privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing during or after the purchase.

Permissions used by the app

  • Show notifications: for your reminders.
  • Run at startup: to keep scheduled reminders after your device has restarted.
  • Keep the device awake briefly: to be able to show a notification on time.
  • Exact times (Android): so that a reminder goes off on the minute. This is only on if you allow it yourself.
  • Internet access: the technology the app is built with requires this permission. The app itself does not connect to the internet and sends nothing.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item in the app. To remove everything at once, clear the app's data in your device settings or uninstall the app. Backup copies you made yourself remain wherever you saved them.

Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Your rights

The maker receives no personal data about you through the app. What you put in the app stays with you, so there is nothing of it held by the maker that you could access, correct or have deleted. For data about your purchase you can also contact Google. If you still have a question, email the address at the top.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated. The date at the top shows when that last happened.
